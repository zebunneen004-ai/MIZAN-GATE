## Freeze policy

Study 1 results, model selection, calibration, alert-budget selection, and claims are historical records. MIZAN-GATE will not overwrite or retrospectively optimize them. Reused code must be copied or generalized with explicit provenance. New results must be written to the MIZAN-GATE repository under new experiment identifiers.

## Reuse classes

- **Frozen reference:** Study 1 reports, final results, figures, and model-selection decisions.
- **Generalize with attribution:** chronological split utilities, metric calculations, provenance utilities, and aligned bootstrap logic.
- **Reimplement:** feature registry, graph-regime controls, availability registry, strict-inductive neural evaluation, and experiment orchestration.
- **Do not restore:** legacy notebooks, hard-coded historical tables, contradictory artifacts, or compliance claims.
